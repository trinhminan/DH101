# About Me

Hello! I'm **Anh**, a Communication major passionate about storytelling, digital exploration, and the intersection of technology and human expression.

## Who I Am

I believe that learning is less like following a linear path and more like embarking on a series of sidequests. Each project, conversation, and experiment teaches me something new about myself, others, and the world we share. I'm drawn to questions that sit at the crossroads of communication, creativity, and critical thinking—especially how digital tools shape the way we make meaning.

## What I Do

As a Communication student, I'm deeply interested in:

- **Digital Storytelling** – How narratives change form across platforms and media
- **Critical Making** – Creating things as a way to understand systems, not just as an end product
- **AI & Ethics** – Examining the implications of generative technologies on authenticity, labor, and sustainability
- **Accessibility & Inclusion** – Designing with everyone in mind, not as an afterthought
- **Cultural Remix** – How meaning emerges when ideas, images, and sounds collide and recombine

## This Project

DH101 is a collection of my "sidequests" in digital humanities—explorations into reverse engineering, visual identity, text analysis, networks, ecology, and futures. Each week, I engage with a different tool or question, documenting both the process and my reflections. This isn't a polished portfolio; it's a thinking space where I work through ideas publicly and imperfectly.

I invite you to explore alongside me.
