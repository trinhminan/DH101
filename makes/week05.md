# Week 5 – GIF & Remix Culture

- Tools used: LTX Studio
- AI prompts (if any): A dark minimal 4–6 second seamless looping video. Every frame includes a clear visual image. Slow, cinematic, emotional, blue-black tones, soft grain, subtle lighting. Clear visible loop.
