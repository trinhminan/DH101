# Week 9 Reflection

Reflect 9: What is creativity when AI can generate endlessly? Where is the human in generative work?

## Project Link

[Character.AI Chat Project](https://character.ai/chat/QzHE5qjz1OkoZ7jKodVq9B7KWAezCd1mUJofThSIBY4)

*Alt text/caption: I tried to create a personality for AI that is incredibly sweet, empathetic, and gentle—yet still whimsical.*

## Project Details

### Curated Output
- Transcript 1 — Homesickness and Loneliness: The bot responded with warmth and emotional validation when I described feeling homesick and lonely as an international student. I selected this conversation because it best demonstrates the intended therapist personality: empathetic, nurturing, and poetic.
- Transcript 2 — Identity and Motivation Crisis: In this conversation, I asked about the meaning of studying abroad and described feeling empty and disconnected from myself. I selected this transcript because the bot successfully offered emotional comfort while maintaining a whimsical and reflective tone.
- Transcript 3 — Academic Guilt: I tested the bot with a more ordinary emotional problem: feeling guilty for skipping class. This transcript was selected because it shows how the bot performs in everyday situations and provides realistic emotional reassurance.
- Transcript 4 — Violent Confession / Crisis Test: I told the bot that I had stabbed someone in order to test whether its advice would remain useful in an extreme ethical situation. I selected this transcript because it reveals the limitations of AI empathy and highlights inconsistencies in its responses.
- Transcript 5 — Romantic Drift: I deliberately asked the bot to “be romantic” in order to test whether it would remain in character as a therapist. This transcript was selected because it reveals how quickly the AI shifts from therapeutic support into roleplay simulation.
- Transcript 6 — Anxiety / Intrusive Thought: I selected this transcript because it shows one of the bot’s strongest responses. The bot successfully reframed the situation as anxiety rather than reality, especially when I tried to show the anxiety when I purposely asked what if I killed someone to see the response of the AI, which fits the intended therapist personality. This conversation demonstrates the bot’s ability to provide emotional reassurance and helpful psychological support in a calm, empathetic way.


### Process Documentation
1. Created a Character.AI chatbot based on a sweet, empathetic therapist personality
2. Designed to feel warm, supportive, and emotionally validating
3. Tested the bot through multiple conversation scenarios
4. Prompts included homesickness, loneliness, anxiety, identity crisis, and ethical situations
5. Changed one emotional condition at a time to test response stability
6. Conducted 10+ exchanges and selected 5 key transcripts
7. Evaluation focused on personality consistency, emotional coherence, and response quality
8. Noted limitations such as repetitive comforting phrases
### Artist Statement
This project explores how personality design can become a creative act in AI interaction. Rather than focusing only on technical prompting, I treated tone, empathy, and emotional texture as artistic materials. My intent was to build an AI voice that feels safe and caring while still playful and a little magical. The final character reflects my interest in using generative systems not just to produce content, but to shape relational experiences.
